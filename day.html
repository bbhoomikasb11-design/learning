Welcome to CampusConnect



CampusConnect is a web application that allows students to connect with each other, share resources, and collaborate on projects. It provides a platform for students to create profiles, join groups, and participate in discussions. With CampusConnect, students can easily find study partners, share notes, and stay updated on campus events.

Feature 1: Student Profiles

Students can create personalized profiles to showcase their interests, skills, and academic background. This feature helps students connect with like-minded peers and find study partners.

Feature 2: Group Collaboration

Students can join or create groups based on their courses, interests, or projects. This feature fosters collaboration and allows students to work together on assignments and share resources.

Feature 3: Campus Events

Students can stay informed about upcoming campus events, workshops, and seminars. This feature helps students engage with the campus community and participate in extracurricular activities.
