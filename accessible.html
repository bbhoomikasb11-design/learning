Inclusive Design

Everyone should be able to experience the web.

Accessibility is not about making a website look different. It is about giving people the flexibility to interact with digital experiences in ways that work best for them.

👁 Visual Adjust contrast and text size for easier reading.
📖 Reading Improve spacing and typography for comfortable reading.
🖱 Motor Reduce unnecessary movement and interaction complexity.
🧠 Cognitive Create calmer interfaces with fewer distractions.
